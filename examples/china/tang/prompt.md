## 提示词

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: Tang-dynasty before/after example for the “pretend ancient life” skill
Input image: Image 1 is the edit target. Preserve the tall portrait crop, compressed telephoto view down a long street, stacked bands of market stalls, dense pedestrian rhythm, dark building walls at both sides, central distant gateway, and repeating pools of light.

Historical setting: the West Market of Chang'an during the cosmopolitan High Tang, eighth century, at night.
Primary request: Transform the modern neon market into a historically coherent Tang ward-market scene painted in ink and mineral color on aged silk, with no modern infrastructure.
Environment conversion: Replace neon signs, electric streetlights, construction tape, metal awnings, concrete apartment blocks, cables, air conditioners, and modern storefronts with low timber shops, tiled roofs with restrained curves, ward walls, cloth awnings, market counters, hanging oil lamps, lanterns, torchlight, banners, and a Tang market gate in the same distant position. Preserve the source's layered stall bands and depth; translate cyan neon into limited cool mineral-green accents surrounded by warm amber lamp light.
People conversion: Keep the crowd density and placement. Dress townspeople in Tang round-collar robes, belts, soft futou, high-waisted skirts, short jackets, and shawls according to role. Include only a few plausible Central or West Asian merchants to express Chang'an's cosmopolitan trade; do not exoticize the entire crowd.
Style/medium: Tang tomb-mural and court-figure-painting visual grammar, strong flowing outlines, lively natural figures, mineral pigments on silk, slightly weathered surface.
Color palette: vermilion, warm amber, ochre, malachite green, muted turquoise, black ink, and aged silk beige.
Text handling: Use blank or tiny subordinate wooden signs and cloth banners. No large legible writing or pseudo-neon characters.
Constraints: Preserve the exact vertical framing, street canyon, gateway position, layered density, crowd rhythm, darkness, and repeating light pattern.
Avoid: electricity, neon, LED, plastic tarps, concrete, steel, cars, modern clothing, Qing queues, Ming official hats, fantasy lantern festival, exaggerated palace eaves, saturated cyberpunk glow, photorealism, watermark, captions, border.
```

## 主要替换

| 现代元素 | 唐代对应物 |
|---|---|
| 霓虹灯和路灯 | 油灯、灯笼和火把 |
| 密集金属摊位 | 木摊、布棚和坊市店铺 |
| 公寓楼和空调外机 | 坊墙、木构店屋与瓦顶 |
| 现代人群 | 盛唐市民与少量异域商旅 |
| 现代门楼 | 长安坊市门 |
