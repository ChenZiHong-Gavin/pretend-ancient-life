```text
Asset type: Han-dynasty before/after example for the “pretend ancient life” skill
Input image: Image 1 is the edit target. Preserve the portrait crop, the three family members around the table, the child centered between the adults, the child's joyful head-back pose and raised chopsticks, the adults' positions at the left and right edges, the tabletop layout, and the strong daylight.

Historical setting: an ordinary Eastern Han household meal in a modest courtyard-side eatery, second century CE.
Primary request: Transform the entire photograph into an Eastern Han painted tomb mural on aged plaster. Keep the family relationship and recognizable pose silhouettes while converting all modern clothing, furniture, containers, signs, appliances, and construction materials.
Subject conversion: Dress the adults in restrained cross-collar right-closing Han robes with cloth belts and simple hair arrangements; dress the child in a plain Han tunic and preserve the exact playful eating gesture. Do not turn the family into nobles, warriors, or terracotta figures.
Environment conversion: Replace the folding table and metal stools with a low black-and-red lacquered dining table, woven sitting mats, and simple low wooden seats. Replace stainless bowls, plastic jars, tissues, gas cylinder, refrigerator, and tiled wall with Han lacquerware, pottery bowls, wooden utensils, an earthen brazier area, timber shelving, and a plastered courtyard wall. Replace the large printed menu with a mostly blank painted wall panel and a few tiny, visually subordinate seal-or-clerical-script marks; no prominent readable text.
Style/medium: Eastern Han tomb mural, mineral and earth pigments on weathered plaster, simplified narrative figures, confident contour lines, flat spatial layers, visible plaster cracks and age.
Color palette: black, vermilion, ochre, earth yellow, gray-green, and warm white.
Constraints: Preserve the source crop, family count, placement, gestures, eye lines, bowl positions, and daylight direction. Make every prop structurally plausible and period coherent.
Avoid: Tang or Song clothing, Qing queues, blue-and-white porcelain, paper lantern displays, folding fans, modern logos, menu typography, plastic, stainless steel, gas cylinders, refrigeration, tiled walls, photography, fantasy palace decor, watermark, border, captions.
```
