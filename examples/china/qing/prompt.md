## 提示词

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: Qing-dynasty before/after example for the “pretend ancient life” skill
Input image: Image 1 is the edit target. Preserve the tall portrait crop, extreme one-point perspective, long parallel transport lines, large covered platform on the right, repeating columns and roof bays, waiting group at right, open pale sky, and distant vanishing point.

Historical setting: an eighteenth-century Qing imperial post station on a major overland route during the Kangxi-Qianlong period.
Primary request: Transform the modern high-speed rail platform into a Qing-era post-road and covered carriage depot painted as a court documentary handscroll with modest linear perspective.
Infrastructure conversion: Replace steel tracks and yellow tactile paving with a broad stone-paved imperial post road containing two long carriage-rut lines that follow the exact original vanishing point. Replace concrete platforms, curved modern canopy, glass, catenary poles, electrical wires, cameras, digital displays, and exit signs with a timber-roofed post-station colonnade, gray-tiled eaves, wooden posts, hitching rails, banner poles, small route plaques, and distant gate structures. Preserve the repeating structural rhythm and open corridor.
People conversion: Keep the waiting group in the same location, size, count, and clustered relationship. Convert them into ordinary Qing travelers and attendants wearing restrained changpao, magua, simple jackets and skirts appropriate to role; men use period-correct Qing hair and headwear. Convert suitcases, bags, and trolley into wooden traveling trunks, cloth bundles, shoulder poles, and a small handcart. Replace phone gestures with reading a folded itinerary or holding luggage.
Transport conversion: Add only a few distant horse-drawn passenger carriages and mounted couriers aligned with the road; do not crowd the foreground.
Style/medium: eighteenth-century Qing court documentary painting, fine ink outline, controlled color on silk, modest Western-influenced linear perspective and subtle modeling, not a photograph.
Color palette: gray tile, warm timber brown, muted teal, ochre, restrained vermilion, pale overcast sky.
Text handling: plaques must be blank or carry tiny subordinate brush marks; no prominent pseudo-writing.
Constraints: Preserve the exact portrait framing, vanishing point, parallel-line geometry, canopy mass, column rhythm, crowd placement, and overcast light.
Avoid: trains, rails, electricity, steel, concrete, glass, digital screens, English text, modern luggage, smartphones, twentieth-century fitted qipao, Republican clothing, Ming official hats, fantasy palace costume, photorealism, watermark, captions, border.
```

## 主要替换

| 现代元素 | 清代对应物 |
|---|---|
| 高铁轨道 | 带车辙的石铺驿路 |
| 现代站台雨棚 | 木柱灰瓦驿站长廊 |
| 接触网和电线 | 驿旗、木杆与远处门楼 |
| 电子屏和出口牌 | 小型木制路牌和告示板 |
| 行李箱和推车 | 木箱笼、布包和手推车 |
