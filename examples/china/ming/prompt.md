## 提示词

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: Ming-dynasty before/after example for the “pretend ancient life” skill
Input image: Image 1 is the edit target. Preserve the 3:2 landscape crop, broad calm harbor, diagonal stone quay entering from the lower right, low waterfront skyline, boat positions, tall crane silhouettes, blue-hour sky, and warm reflections on the water.

Historical setting: a prosperous early-to-mid Ming commercial harbor in Jiangnan during evening cargo activity.
Primary request: Transform the entire modern harbor into a coherent Ming maritime trade scene painted as a precise professional narrative handscroll in ink and color on silk.
Environment conversion: Replace apartment and office blocks with riverside warehouses, guild halls, inns, and multi-story timber shops with gray tiled roofs, preserving each mass and the skyline rhythm. Replace the concrete quay with a dressed-stone wharf, stepped landings, timber bollards, crates, tea chests, ceramic jars, bundled textiles, porters, and handcarts. Replace modern cranes with tall junk masts, rigging, a timber dockside lifting frame, and a watchtower that preserve the source's vertical accents.
Boat conversion: Replace motor ferries and workboats with proportionally placed Ming river junks, coastal trading vessels, and oared ferries. Keep the water open and do not overcrowd it.
Lighting: Replace electric windows and streetlights with sparse warm oil lanterns, retaining their reflection positions and the cool blue-hour atmosphere.
Style/medium: meticulous Ming professional painting, fine architectural and ship linework, ink and controlled mineral color on aged silk, documentary commerce rather than fantasy spectacle.
Color palette: indigo-blue dusk, warm lantern amber, gray tile, ink brown, restrained vermilion, mineral green accents.
Constraints: Preserve the original shoreline, water-to-land ratio, boat positions, skyline masses, crane/mast verticals, reflections, and evening mood.
Avoid: engines, modern ferries, glass, concrete, tower cranes, streetlights, cars, shipping containers, steamships, European harbor architecture, Qing queues, fantasy treasure fleet, excessive imperial dragons, photorealism, watermark, captions, border, prominent pseudo-writing.
```

## 主要替换

| 现代元素 | 明代对应物 |
|---|---|
| 轮渡和工作艇 | 明式河船、商船和摆渡船 |
| 港口起重机 | 桅杆、木起重架和望楼 |
| 公寓和写字楼 | 仓房、会馆、客栈与木构商铺 |
| 混凝土码头 | 石码头、台阶和木系船柱 |
| 电灯 | 稀疏油灯与水面暖色倒影 |
