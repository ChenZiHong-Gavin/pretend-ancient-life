## 提示词

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: Southern Song before/after example for the “pretend ancient life” skill
Input images: Image 1 is the edit target.
Primary request: Transform this misty mountain hiking photograph into a lyrical Southern Song winter mountain journey painted in ink and very light color on aged silk.
Composition: Preserve the tall portrait crop, enormous pale mist-filled negative space, layered mountain silhouettes, bare trees rising from the lower half, diagonal dirt path entering from the lower right, rustic fence rhythm, small roof partly hidden near the lower center, and exactly four travelers clustered at the lower-right bend.
Travelers: Keep exactly four people with the same relative positions, walking directions, small scale, and spacing. Replace modern jackets, bright synthetic colors, hiking boots, and backpacks with plain Song travel robes, short outer coats, cloth leggings, straw capes or muted cloaks, cloth headwraps, straw sandals, and practical bundles. Translate backpacks into wrapped cloth baggage, a shoulder basket, a rolled umbrella, or a small book bundle while keeping each silhouette and load-bearing posture. They are ordinary travelers with one scholar-like figure and attendants, not warriors or immortals.
Landscape conversion: Replace modern fencing, plastic scraps, and trail debris with a rough woven bamboo fence, weathered wooden gate, natural stone edging, and a small tiled mountain rest hut. Preserve the winter-bare vegetation and fog, but shape the distant ridges with layered ink wash, textured rocks, and controlled blank silk for clouds.
Style/medium: Southern Song lyrical landscape album leaf, Ma-Xia corner-composition sensibility, expressive ax-cut-like texture strokes used sparingly, broad mist washes, fine figures, restrained mineral color on aged silk. Use the tradition as visual grammar, not a copy of any existing painting.
Color palette: ink gray, warm paper cream, pale brown, muted blue-green, tiny restrained cinnabar accents only on travel gear.
Constraints: Preserve the exact vertical framing, negative-space ratio, four-person count, path geometry, figure cluster, tree silhouettes, roof position, mist layers, and quiet overcast light.
Avoid: modern backpacks, synthetic jackets, plastic, trekking poles, sneakers, bright red outdoor gear, fantasy monks, martial-arts heroes, temples in the clouds, excessive blue-green mineral color, photographic fog effects, watermark, caption, border, prominent writing.
```

## 主要替换

| 现代元素 | 宋代对应物 |
|---|---|
| 冲锋衣和徒步鞋 | 素色行旅袍、短褐、绑腿和草履 |
| 登山包 | 布包、肩篮、伞卷与书囊 |
| 现代围栏 | 编竹篱和木门 |
| 步道垃圾 | 自然石块、枯叶与土坡 |
| 现代山屋 | 小型瓦顶歇脚亭 |
