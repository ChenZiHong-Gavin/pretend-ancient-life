## 提示词

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: Northern Song before/after example for the “pretend ancient life” skill
Input images: Image 1 is the edit target.
Primary request: Transform this modern Shanghai river-shipping photograph into a busy Northern Song grain-transport reach of the Bian River outside Bianjing, painted as a precise urban-and-river handscroll in ink and light color on aged silk.
Water and vessels: Preserve the 3:2 landscape crop, low eye level over the water, foreground wake, open-water proportion, the large foreground barge moving right, left middle-distance vessel, central cargo vessel, right-side cluster of passenger boats, and their relative sizes and directions. Replace each powered steel vessel with a structurally plausible Northern Song flat-bottom grain boat, covered cargo barge, oared ferry, or timber river junk matched to the original function and position. Replace engines, cabins, tires, railings, antennas, flags, painted numbers, and containers with timber hulls, bamboo mat shelters, sculling oars, tow ropes, masts, folded sails, cargo sacks, grain baskets, and a few working boatmen.
Waterfront conversion: Replace the entire glass-and-concrete skyline with a dense but plausible Song waterfront: layered two- and three-story timber warehouses, inns, guild halls, tiled shop roofs, wharf gates, watch pavilions, embankment stairs, and distant low hills. Preserve the source skyline rhythm and central opening without turning ordinary buildings into palaces or implausible wooden skyscrapers.
Style/medium: Northern Song gongbi and jiehua river-commerce painting, exact vessel construction, fine architectural ruling, controlled ink outlines, pale mineral colors on silk, documentary activity reminiscent of Northern Song urban scroll grammar without copying a specific composition.
Lighting/mood: Preserve the clear cool daylight, pale sky, warm sun on portions of the distant waterfront, blue-gray water, wakes, and atmospheric depth.
Color palette: blue-gray ink wash, timber brown, aged silk beige, muted ochre, celadon, indigo, restrained vermilion accents.
Text handling: No modern logos or numbers; use blank or tiny subordinate boat marks and plaques only.
Constraints: Preserve the framing, water-to-skyline ratio, exact vessel hierarchy, travel directions, wakes, horizon, and focal depth. Remove every modern propulsion and building material.
Avoid: engines, steel hulls, container ships, ferries, rubber tires, radar, antennas, glass, concrete, skyscrapers, neon, modern flags, Qing war junks, giant treasure ships, fantasy harbor, excessive pagodas, photorealism, watermark, caption, border, prominent pseudo-writing.
```

## 主要替换

| 现代元素 | 宋代对应物 |
|---|---|
| 机动驳船和轮渡 | 粮船、篷船、木驳和摆渡舟 |
| 发动机、雷达和轮胎 | 桨橹、纤绳、竹篷和木质护舷 |
| 集装箱与现代货舱 | 粮袋、竹筐和成捆货物 |
| 摩天楼群 | 仓房、客栈、会馆和码头门楼 |
| 现代滨水设施 | 石驳岸、台阶、木码头和纤道 |
