## 提示词

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: Northern Song before/after example for the “pretend ancient life” skill
Input images: Image 1 is the edit target.
Primary request: Transform this modern Beijing pedestrian shopping street into a historically coherent Northern Song dynasty avenue in Bianjing around the early twelfth century, rendered as a refined gongbi-and-jiehua urban handscroll painting on aged silk.
Scene conversion: Preserve the broad straight avenue, central vanishing point, open sky, dense crowd rhythm, foreground pairs seen from behind, central vehicle position, and tall building masses on both sides. Replace modern malls, glass windows, LED billboards, shop logos, street furniture, bare modern facades, surveillance equipment, and the distant modern dome with layered timber-frame shops, inns, guild halls, restrained tiled roofs, cloth awnings, market stalls, a distant city gate tower, wooden plaques, wine banners, and small vendor canopies. Roof curves must remain restrained and functional, not fantasy architecture.
People conversion: Keep approximately the same crowd count, placement, walking directions, foreground scale, and social groupings. Dress merchants, porters, shoppers, women, children, and clerks in role-appropriate Northern Song robes, beizi, skirts, cloth belts, futou, simple caps, and tied hair. Preserve the two foreground groups and their facing directions. Replace the small central electric shuttle with a compact covered donkey cart at the same scale and position; replace modern handbags and shopping bags with cloth bundles, baskets, wrapped parcels, shoulder poles, or account books according to function.
Style/medium: Northern Song urban genre painting, meticulous fine-line drawing, accurate ruled architecture, restrained ink and light mineral color on silk, lively documentary detail, visually reminiscent of the scene grammar of Zhang Zeduan’s urban scroll without copying its composition.
Lighting/mood: Preserve the clear daylight, long unobstructed sightline, warm sun on the right-side trees and buildings, and slightly cool pale sky.
Color palette: aged silk beige, ink brown, muted ochre, celadon, indigo, restrained vermilion, pale mineral green.
Text handling: Make plaques and banners blank or use only tiny visually subordinate brush marks; no large readable pseudo-writing.
Constraints: Preserve the exact 3:2 landscape framing, camera height, central perspective, road width, crowd density, main figure placement, and left/right building balance. Remove every modern object and adjust all hand poses naturally when replacing phones or bags.
Avoid: cars, electric shuttles, glass curtain walls, concrete malls, LEDs, neon, billboards, printed ads, modern type, street cameras, plastic, modern winter jackets, Qing queues, Tang court dress, Ming official hats, exaggerated eaves, fantasy palace skyline, photorealism, watermark, caption, border.
```

## 主要替换

| 现代元素 | 宋代对应物 |
|---|---|
| 商场、玻璃店面和广告屏 | 木构商铺、客栈、布棚和木牌 |
| 中央电动接驳车 | 同位置的小型驴车 |
| 购物袋和手提包 | 布包、竹篮、包裹与挑担 |
| 现代冬装 | 北宋市民袍服、褙子和素色裙装 |
| 远处现代穹顶 | 汴京城门楼 |
