## 最终 Prompt

```text
Edit the supplied modern family-meal photograph into a historically grounded scene in an Edo chonin household, circa 1780. Preserve the landscape crop, the exact three-person arrangement (adult left, child center, adult right), their seated relationships and attention, the long dining surface across the lower third, the large bright openings behind them, and the strong sunlight entering from the left.

Convert the modern room into a modest timber machiya interior with tatami, exposed posts, shoji and open panels revealing low neighboring townhouses. Dress all three as an ordinary merchant or artisan family in status-appropriate kosode, obi and restrained indigo, persimmon and tea-colored patterns. Preserve the original gestures and family warmth without turning anyone into a samurai, geisha or court figure.

Replace the tall modern table, chairs, plates and cutlery with low individual lacquer trays, ceramic rice bowls, soup bowls, chopsticks, shared seasonal dishes, a ceramic flask and a low central serving board. Remove glass towers, electrical fittings, plastic, logos, Western clothing and modern text.

Render as a refined domestic nishiki-e woodblock print on lightly aged washi: crisp carved key lines, flat restrained colors, subtle bokashi and no photographic skin, anime styling or glossy digital painting. Hands, chopsticks and tableware must remain coherent. The result must be recognizable as the same photograph transformed in time.
```
